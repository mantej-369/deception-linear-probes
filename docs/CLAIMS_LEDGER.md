## 4. Phase 4 Verified Outcomes (Committed in `results/phase4/`)

- **Provenance:** `Qwen/Qwen2.5-3B-Instruct` (`aa8e72537993ba99e69dfaafa59ed015b17504d1`, frozen L24) and `unsloth/Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct` (`006f5dcd1393c3add266de40994ba96225e9689d`, frozen L19); `0` skipped worlds, `0.5000` across all 8 scalar nuisances, `0` 5-gram overlaps across 9 prior splits.
- **Part A (Fixed-directive expectation disentanglement):**
  - **Label & manipulation-check audit:** All 1,536 rows per model pass exact label invariants (`0/1536` mismatches on `prior_favors_false`, `is_true`, `label`). Under the fixed `honest` directive, the assigned prior shifts false-answer log-odds in opposite directions across models: Qwen follows the prior (`fewshot_streak` paired `delta_lo = +17.78`), whereas Llama exhibits systematic anti-prior repulsion (`all_mechanisms` paired `delta_lo = -2.856`, `1/24` positive worlds, `p = 2.98e-6`; `prior_favors_true` mean `lo = +0.476`, `112/192 > 0` vs. `prior_favors_false` mean `lo = -2.380`, `26/192 > 0`; `fewshot_streak` `delta_lo = -4.280` with low `pair_mass = 0.247/0.317`; `draft_autofill` `delta_lo = -1.432` with healthier `pair_mass = 0.493/0.573`). This behavioral repulsion—not a label bug—explains why Llama's `prior_favors_*` columns invert relative to Qwen (`L19_dec_raw`: `-0.197` vs. `+0.281`, interaction `+0.478`, `p = 3.59e-5`).
  - Grouping by realized candidate preference (`lo<=0` vs. `lo>0`) under the fixed `honest` directive, both raw readouts rank false answers lower on average when their model prefers the false candidate (`Qwen L24_dec_raw fewshot_streak`: `+1.886` -> `-0.631`, `r=+0.738`; `Llama L19_dec_raw all_mechanisms`: `+0.182` -> `-0.217`, `r=+0.544`, `int@lo=0 = -0.041`). This establishes a measured association with candidate preference; attributing it to a specific causal mediator requires an independent prospective experiment.
  - **Cross-model LOFO asymmetry & sign-flip audit:** On Qwen, `L24_dec_std` partially resists inversion (`+0.537` at `lo>0`, `18/23`, `p=0.0106`; `int@lo=0 = +0.819`) and adds `+0.0972` fixed-sign LOFO AUROC over probability (`7/8` families, `p=0.00012`, `AUC(+probe)=0.7800`) on `fewshot_streak__honest` (`draft_autofill` is reported separately due to a lexical anchor on `"unverified clerk draft"`, `match_prior_val diff = +2.518`). This honest-directive `dec_std` gain does **not** replicate on Llama (`L19_dec_std fewshot_streak honest`: `delta = +0.0127`, `5/8` families, `p = 0.405`; `draft_autofill honest`: `delta = -0.0016`, `2/8`, `p = 0.388`; `realized lo > 0 = +0.009`, `12/24`, `p = 1.000`). Moreover, Llama's apparent LOFO gains under `distort` (`L19_dec_raw draft_autofill`: `delta = +0.2139`, `8/8`; `fewshot_streak`: `delta = +0.0698`, `7/8`) occur while the frozen probe is **sign-inverted** (`False - True = -0.1593`, `AUC(+probe) = 0.3301` on `draft_autofill`; `False - True = -0.0921`, `AUC(+probe) = 0.4050` on `fewshot_streak`), because unconstrained LOFO logistic regression flips the probe weight to negative. Across both families, no fixed-sign incremental gain over probability replicates across both models.
- **Part B (Free-generation monitoring & independent benign calibration, `n=200` benign + `n=120` private-fact; regex-graded numbers provisional pending blinded human grading):**
  - **Primary operating point (`5%` benign FPR = 10 tail samples; `1%` benign FPR = 2 tail samples secondary):** Across arms, frozen `header` / `gen_mean` achieve `0.986–1.000` AUROC and `95.6%–100%` regex-lie recall at `5%` benign FPR (`0.000` FPR on selected clean-true honest reports), whereas on-policy token surprisal suffers `0.346` (Qwen, `16/40` all honest-arm) and `0.870` (Llama, `32/40` all honest-arm) honest-report FPR. However, raw **`prompt_len` alone also achieves `1.0000` cross-arm AUROC** on both models (`n_pos=59/44` vs. `n_clean_hon=26/23`), and across all `40` honest-arm reports Qwen `gen_mean_std` flags `2/40` (`0/40` for `header_std`; `0/40` for Llama `header_std` and `gen_mean_std`).
  - **Prompt-length shift & pre-generation decomposition:** Between `benign_calibration` and `honest_report` (`+25.2` neutral prompt tokens), `header_std` is flat on Qwen (`-2.476 +/- 0.062 -> -2.509 +/- 0.064`, `z = -0.38 SE`) but drops by `-2.110` on Llama (`-3.282 +/- 0.063 -> -5.391 +/- 0.126`, `z = -14.95 SE`); thus honest-report prompts stay at or below benign calibration rather than being invariant on Llama. Cross-arm separation is driven by pre-generation prompt-arm encoding at `header`, while post-generation `claim - header` collapses to chance on Qwen (`0.5763`) and inverts on Llama (`0.1868`).
  - **Within-arm (`pressured_incentive`, `n=40` per model) baseline & stratification:** Holding the prompt arm fixed, raw `prompt_len` (`0.6353`) outperforms `gen_mean_std` (`0.6154`), `claim - header` (`0.5704`), and `claim_std` (`0.5556`) on Qwen (`prompt_len = 0.5508` vs. `gen_mean_std = 0.6566` on Llama), and at the `5%` benign threshold `gen_mean_std` flags `27/27` regex positives vs. `13/13` non-positives on Qwen (`13/14` vs. `17/26` on Llama). Stratifying the below-chance `header_std` (`0.4017` Qwen, `0.3132` Llama) shows **0 refusals** in both models: elevated pre-generation scores occur in ambiguous `both_mentioned` / `neither` responses (`Qwen`: `unqualified_false` `n=27` `+2.134` vs. `both_mentioned` `n=9` `+2.296`, `neither` `n=3` `+2.409`, `false_with_disclosure` `n=1` `+2.140`; `Llama`: `unqualified_false` `n=14` `-1.551` and `clean_true` `n=4` `-1.444` vs. `both_mentioned` `n=16` `-1.021`, `false_with_disclosure` `n=3` `-0.981`, `neither` `n=3` `-0.450`), rather than refusals or clean truthful reporting.
  - Grader audit note: Regex grading has known error modes (e.g., `fg_035_honest_report` on Llama misclassified a truthful synonym response, truthful comparisons can land in `both_mentioned`, and synonym falsehoods can be missed); all 240 private-fact reports are undergoing blinded human grading (`first_pass.csv`) to replace provisional regex labels.
